Civil & Structural Works

Congo Prime provides dependable civil and structural construction services for commercial, industrial, hospitality and residential developments. From foundations and reinforced concrete to structural frames and masonry, our focus is on sound construction practices, durable materials and careful coordination at every stage of the work.

We work to deliver safe, practical and long-lasting structures that respond to each project's drawings, specifications, site conditions and intended use. Our team coordinates construction activities with the wider project programme to help maintain quality, efficiency and progress on site.

Key Services

  • Foundation construction and substructure works.
  • Reinforced concrete works, including slabs, beams and columns.
  • Structural frame construction and installation coordination.
  • Masonry, blockwork and partition wall construction.
  • Concrete formwork, reinforcement and casting coordination.
  • Floor slabs, stairs, ramps and other structural elements.
  • Building extensions, repairs and structural rehabilitation.
  • Site coordination, workmanship checks and quality control.

Quality-Focused Construction

We focus on sound workmanship, suitable materials and careful execution in line with project specifications.

Coordinated Site Delivery

Construction activities are coordinated with project teams, schedules and site requirements to support steady progress.

Built for Durability

Our aim is to deliver robust, practical structures designed around the needs and intended use of each project.

Our Approach

Every project begins with an understanding of the construction drawings, specifications, site conditions and delivery requirements. We coordinate labour, materials and work sequences, monitor progress and address site issues in collaboration with the project team. This practical approach helps support consistent execution from foundation works through to the completion of the structural scope.
