Construction and Infrastructure Development in Lubumbashi

Lubumbashi, the mining and economic hub of the Democratic Republic of Congo’s Haut-Katanga Province, continues to attract attention for transport infrastructure, housing, commercial property and wider urban development. The status of individual projects varies: some have reported construction or rehabilitation work, while others remain proposed, announced or in planning.

A major public project is the modernization of Luano International Airport. Announced works have included plans for a new passenger terminal and improvements to airport infrastructure. The Lubumbashi–Likasi section of National Road 1 (RN1) has also been a focus of rehabilitation work, with reports of final acceptance in 2025.

Private-sector developments such as River City and Luano City have been presented as mixed-use projects intended to bring together residential, commercial and other facilities. City of David has also been announced as a large-scale new-city development; an announcement alone should not be interpreted as confirmation that every phase is under construction.

A proposed Karema–Lubumbashi railway has been discussed in the context of regional transport planning between the DRC and Tanzania. If advanced, projects of this kind could support trade and connectivity, but proposals should be distinguished from funded or active construction. The following list highlights developments to monitor across Lubumbashi and the wider region.

Major Projects to Watch

  • Modernization of Luano International Airport
  • City of David new-city development
  • Lubumbashi–Likasi road rehabilitation and widening
  • River City residential and commercial development
  • Luano City multi-use development
  • Proposed Karema–Lubumbashi railway line
  • Kasumbalesa–Sakania and Haut-Katanga corridor toll-road proposals
  • National airport programme, including the Lubumbashi hub component
  • Commercial and retail real-estate infill in central Lubumbashi
  • Lwambo–Mitwaba–Manono and other regional feeder-road improvements

What This Could Mean for the Region

Improved roads, airport facilities and urban services can help connect businesses, workers and communities. New residential and commercial developments may also increase demand for reliable utilities, safe access, durable buildings and well-coordinated construction. The practical impact will depend on funding, implementation, maintenance and the delivery of each project phase.

Project-status note: This article is an overview, not a confirmation that every listed project is approved, funded or under construction. Readers should check announcements from relevant public authorities, project owners and contractors for the latest verified status before making investment or planning decisions.
