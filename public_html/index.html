Building excellence through

Congo Prime is a young, dynamic construction company based in Lubumbashi, DRC, delivering industrial, commercial, hospitality, and residential projects with speed and precision. Unburdened by legacy practices, we combine agility with disciplined execution to meet modern construction demands across the region.

Our leadership blends energetic professionals with seasoned industry veterans, backed by diversified business interests across Congo and neighbouring countries. Our core team of engineers, supervisors, architects, and designers ensures every project is technically sound, efficiently managed, and aligned with client objectives.
