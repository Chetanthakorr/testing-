Civil & Structural Works

We deliver dependable civil and structural works that form the foundation of safe, durable construction projects. From site preparation and foundations to concrete and structural frameworks, our team focuses on quality workmanship, coordination and compliance with project specifications.cerm-ex+1

Key Services

  • - Site clearing, excavation, and earthworks
  • - Foundations, piling, and ground beams
  • - Reinforced concrete works, including slabs, columns, and beams
  • - Structural steel fabrication and erection
  • - Masonry, retaining walls, and other structural elements
  • - Drainage, roads, paving, and associated site infrastructure
  • - Construction supervision, quality checks, and coordination with project teams
