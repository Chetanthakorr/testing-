Building Excellence

At Congo Prime we believe that every project must be built to last, with uncompromising quality, integrity, and respect for people and planet.

Congo Prime is a young, dynamic construction company based in Lubumbashi, DRC, delivering industrial, commercial, hospitality, residential and infrastructure projects with speed and precision.

Our leadership blends energetic professionals with seasoned industry veterans, backed by diversified business interests across Congo and neighbouring countries.

Our core team of engineers, supervisors, architects, and designers ensures every project is technically sound, efficiently managed, and aligned with client objectives. Through careful planning, meticulous attention to detail, and a focus on cost transparency, we deliver results that exceed expectations whether it is a small single project or large scale turnkey projects

Our vision is to set the benchmark for quality and safety in the construction projects we undertake, using innovation and disciplined execution to turn ambitious plans into landmark assets.
